Prison headroom, England and Wales

Headroom means the bulletin's total-estate Useable Operational Capacity minus total-estate Population. Model version v3, forecast from the 24 Aug 2026 bulletin, headroom 2,094.

The claim, as registered

Headroom in the England and Wales prison estate, as published in the Ministry of Justice weekly prison population bulletin for Monday 28 Sep 2026, will be 2,076, with an 80% interval of 1,505 to 2,672.

Every line on this track takes that wording with its own target, forecast and interval. The wording is fixed at registration and is never edited.

Scoring rule

  1. Field. Total-estate Useable Operational Capacity minus total-estate Population in the MoJ weekly bulletin, equal to the bulletin's own Headroom row. The figure as first published is used; later MoJ revisions are logged but do not change the score.
  2. Resolving bulletin. The bulletin whose reference date equals the target date. If no bulletin exists for that date, the first bulletin after it resolves the line, and that bulletin's date is recorded as the resolving date.
  3. Error equals forecast minus outcome, in people, sign kept.
  4. Interval hit means the outcome falls within the 80% interval, inclusive.
  5. Closer means a smaller absolute error than the named baseline, per line. Across the track, the model is said to beat a baseline only when its running mean absolute error is lower over at least 13 resolved lines.
  6. Policy events between bulletins have no effect. A release tranche, a recall change, a capacity measure or anything else occurring between bulletins does not alter the forecast or the score. Events are logged for context only.
  7. Definitional change. If the MoJ changes the meaning of useable operational capacity, the line is still scored against the published figure and the change is logged. The target is the published number.
