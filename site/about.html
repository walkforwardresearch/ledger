About

What this is

Walkforward Research is an independent research project. It publishes forecasts about British public institutions before the outcome is known, and scores them in public afterwards against the official or market forecast each one set out to beat.

Privacy

This site is a set of static files. It sets no cookies, has no accounts and no mailing list, and asks you for nothing.

The host counts page views in aggregate so we know roughly how many people read something. That count uses no cookies and does not identify anyone.

If you email enquiries@walkforwardresearch.com, your message and address are used to reply to you and for nothing else. They are not sold, shared or added to any list. Ask and they will be deleted.
